    How it was built and evaluated

    Ask the filings

    Questions about the 10-K annual reports of NVIDIA, AMD, Micron, Broadcom, Qualcomm and Marvell, fiscal years 2023–2026. Every answer comes only from the filings and cites its sources. If the reports don’t say, it answers “Not in the filings.”

    Name the company and fiscal year for the best results. Runs on a free server: the first question after a quiet spell can take about a minute while it wakes up.

    It can’t forecast, look up share prices or news, or answer follow-up questions: each question is answered on its own.
